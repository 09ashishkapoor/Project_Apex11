The practiceThree things that shape a sitting.

Built for the counting surface, not the dashboard.

Count the way you sit

Tap each mantra, chant with the audio, sit for a set time, or let the mala count itself. Four ways in, one practice.

The mala, not a progress bar

108 beads ride the edge of the darshan and fill one by one. When the mala closes, you offer it — the beads go to her.

It stays on your device

Your ledger lives in your browser. No account, no sync, no analytics. Export the whole record as text whenever you like.

The altarsEach with its own chant.

Four names, four darshans, four mantras.

Every altar carries its own darshan and its own recorded mantra.

Adya Mahakali

ॐ आद्यायै नमः

At the lotus feet of the primordial Mother.

Batuk Bhairav

ॐ बटुक भैरवाय नमः

Guardian of swift grace and fearless protection.

Swarnaakarshana

ॐ श्री स्वर्णाकर्षण भैरवाय नमः

For radiance, dignified prosperity, and clear intent.

Skanda Bhairav

ॐ सर्वान्भवाय नमः

For disciplined courage and focused tapas.

The appNothing to learn.

A surface for counting, a ledger for keeping.

InstallOne visit, then it is yours.

Add it to your phone in two taps.

MANTRINI is a website that installs like an app. After the first visit it opens without a connection and keeps working at the altar.

Android Chrome, Edge, Brave

  1. Open Open MANTRINI above.
  2. Tap the Install prompt, or open the menu ⋮ and choose Add to Home screen.
  3. It appears with the trikoṇa icon, and opens full screen.

iPhone & iPad Safari

  1. Open Open MANTRINI in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Launch it from the Home Screen, not from a bookmark.

On iPhone, please install rather than bookmark. Safari may clear a website's stored data after seven days of no use; an app added to the Home Screen is kept. Your ledger is only ever on your own device, so a backup from the Ledger screen is the safest habit.
