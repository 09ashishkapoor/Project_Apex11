Four ways to count

Count the way you sit.

Tap

Count with your thumb One tap, one mantra. The chant stays silent.

Audio

Chant along The mantra sounds once for every count.

Timer

Sit for a set time Five to sixty minutes. The chant keeps the sitting.

Auto

Let the mala count itself The chant loops and the beads fill on their own.

One practice, four ways in.
