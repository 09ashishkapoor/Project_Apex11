The Ledger

A printed sadhana register, not a stack of cards.

Japa is a counted thing kept daily, so this direction makes the app one printed page: a single reading column hanging off the left margin, hairline rules where the other directions would put cards, and a display serif that lets the count be the ornament. Warm paper, ink, one oxide mark. The margin is left unclaimed on purpose — the air is part of the composition.
